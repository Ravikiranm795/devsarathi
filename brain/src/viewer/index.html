    What you are looking at

    • Each district is a folder. Each plot is a file.
    • Each block is a function, method or class. Its height is the measure you pick under View options.
    • Colour is the kind of symbol. Select a block to see who calls it and what it calls.
    • Orange arcs leave the selected block: what it calls. Blue arcs arrive at it: who calls it. They draw on in the direction of the call.
